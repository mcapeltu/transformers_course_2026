General description of the block

The Transformer block covers sequence modelling tasks, embeddings and positional encoding, self-attention and scaled dot-product attention, Queries, Keys and Values, Transformer block architecture, the training pipeline of Transformer models, autoregressive Transformers and causal masking, and a basic implementation of attention in PyTorch.

This completes the theoretical part of the course and prepares students for the mini-project implementation phase.

Theory material

The lecture material introduces the three core sequence-modelling tasks, embeddings, positional information, scaled dot-product attention, Transformer blocks, training with cross-entropy, and autoregressive generation with causal masking.
