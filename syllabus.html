Topics included

  • Sequence modelling tasks: sequence labelling, sequence classification, and autoregressive generation.
  • Embeddings and positional encoding.
  • Self-attention and scaled dot-product attention.
  • Queries, Keys and Values.
  • Transformer block structure: attention, feed-forward, residuals, and layer normalization.
  • Training pipeline with logits, softmax, cross-entropy, and gradient descent.
  • PyTorch implementation of attention.
  • Autoregressive generation and causal masking.

Why this block matters

Students move from the conceptual understanding of sequence models to a concrete implementation of a minimal character-level Transformer. The emphasis is not only on formulas but also on tensor shapes, masking logic, and the relationship between the mathematics and the PyTorch code.

Core equations

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax((QK^T) / sqrt(d_k)) V
Masked attention: scores[j > i] = -inf before softmax

Recommended study order

  1. Understand embeddings and positional information.
  2. Study attention as weighted aggregation.
  3. Understand why Q/K/V are separate projections.
  4. Review scaling by sqrt(d_k).
  5. Study multi-head attention and the Transformer block.
  6. Connect the formulas to PyTorch operations.
  7. Study autoregressive masking and next-token prediction.
  8. Run the mini-project notebook.
